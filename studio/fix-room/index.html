Tonal balance against a typical club master

Bars inside the shaded band are normal. A bar above it means that range is heavier than usual, below it means thinner. The reference is a rough guide for house, techno and drum and bass, so treat it as a second opinion and trust your ears.

Waveform and audition window

The highlighted 30 seconds is what you audition. It starts on the busiest part of the track, where shimmer piles up. Click anywhere on the waveform to move it, for example to a breakdown to check the hiss.
